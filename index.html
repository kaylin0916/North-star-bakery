Warm Bread, Made for the Neighborhood

North Star Bakery offers handcrafted breads, pastries, and cakes in a warm, community-centered setting. We welcome local customers, commuters, families, and event planners looking for fresh baked goods and friendly service.

What We're Known For

Our bakery focuses on small-batch baking, dependable classics, and seasonal favorites. From crusty loaves to pastries and celebration cakes, each item is prepared with care and meant to be shared.
