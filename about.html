Our Story

North Star Bakery grew from a simple idea: create a neighborhood bakery where fresh food and familiar faces belong together. Our team prepares small batches throughout the day so customers can stop in for everyday favorites or plan ahead for family gatherings and events.

Our Values

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We choose ingredients with quality, consistency, and community in mind. Whenever practical, we look for dependable regional suppliers and build recipes around simple ingredients that let the finished bread and pastry speak for itself.

Meet the Team

Our bakers, counter staff, and order team work together from the first morning bake through the final pickup of the day. We value clear communication, careful preparation, and friendly service.
